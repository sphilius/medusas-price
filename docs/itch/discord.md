## 1. Jam submission post (Jamference AI Game Jam Hack 3 · #submissions)

🐍 **Medusa's Price** is in! https://kingriot.itch.io/medusas-price

You're Medusa, hunted by heroes. Your gaze turns them to marble, and the statues become your platforms. But every petrification ages your portrait (a Caravaggio-style shield, your only health bar) from Defiant to Stone.

🏺 Theme *Great Works of Art*: the whole game is a black-figure Greek vase painting, and the run ends with your run painted onto an amphora by AI (with a letter check so no fake Greek sneaks on, and a hand-made fallback offline).
🪞 Perseus watches how you fight and adapts his shield window and attack side.
🚫 No text anywhere. Everything is told with colour, icons and pictures.

Browser, about 10 minutes, keyboard or touch. Would love to hear where you got stuck!

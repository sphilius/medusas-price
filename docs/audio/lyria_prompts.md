# Lyria 3 music prompts: Medusa's Price

Five loops, one per portrait stage. The game plays the loop for Medusa's current stage and crossfades over 2 s when the stage changes. A pool fills the music back in.

**Every track must share key, tempo and instruments**, so the crossfades sound like one piece thinning out, not five songs:

| | |
|---|---|
| Key / mode | D Phrygian (dark, ancient Greek colour) |
| Tempo | 84 BPM, 4/4 |
| Palette | kithara/lyre (plucked), aulos (double reed), frame drum, low drone, wordless female choir hums |
| Length | the longest clip Lyria allows; it will loop |
| Rule | **no words, no lyrics** (instrumental and wordless hums only) |

## How to use

1. 🎛️ Paste one prompt into Lyria 3 and generate.
2. 🔁 Listen for a clean ending. If the ending fades or stops abruptly, regenerate; a loop needs a steady last bar.
3. 💾 Save as `stage_1.ogg` … `stage_5.ogg` (MP3 or WAV is fine too; I'll convert) and drop them in `audio/music/`.
4. ✅ Tell me "music saved" and I'll trim them to loop and wire them in.

## Stage 1: Defiant (full, proud)

> Instrumental ancient Greek war music loop, 84 BPM, 4/4, key of D Phrygian. A proud, defiant mood: a bright plucked kithara melody leads, an aulos double reed answers it, steady driving frame drums and a low bronze drone underneath, a wordless female choir humming long notes. Full ensemble, confident and fierce, the sound of a gorgon who is still strong. Seamless loop, steady to the last bar, no fade-out. No vocals with words, no lyrics.

## Stage 2: Rage (driving, harsher)

> Instrumental ancient Greek war music loop, 84 BPM, 4/4, key of D Phrygian. An angry, relentless mood: the same plucked kithara and aulos double reed, now playing short aggressive phrases, frame drums hit harder with syncopated accents, the low drone grows rougher and buzzing, a wordless female choir hums in tense clusters. Slightly fewer melodic notes than a full arrangement, more rhythm. Seamless loop, steady to the last bar, no fade-out. No vocals with words, no lyrics.

## Stage 3: Grief (thinning, mournful)

> Instrumental ancient Greek lament loop, 84 BPM, 4/4, key of D Phrygian. A mournful, grieving mood: a slow plucked kithara melody with space between notes, a lonely aulos double reed playing a falling lament, only a soft frame drum pulse on the first beat, a low drone, a single wordless female voice humming softly. Sparse and sad, the ensemble has thinned out. Seamless loop, steady to the last bar, no fade-out. No vocals with words, no lyrics.

## Stage 4: Exhaustion (sparse, heavy)

> Instrumental ancient Greek lament loop, 84 BPM, 4/4, key of D Phrygian. An exhausted, heavy mood: a low sustained drone, a very sparse plucked kithara playing a few tired notes, a breathy aulos double reed sighing occasionally, a faint frame drum heartbeat every other bar, distant wordless humming. Very quiet and empty, like strength running out. Seamless loop, steady to the last bar, no fade-out. No vocals with words, no lyrics.

## Stage 5: Stone (almost silent, cold)

> Instrumental dark ambient loop, 84 BPM, 4/4, key of D Phrygian. A cold, petrified mood: only a deep stone-like drone and a few isolated plucked kithara harmonics ringing out like cracks, a faint low frame drum heartbeat slowing, a ghostly breath of wordless humming far away. Nearly silent, frozen, the sound of turning to stone. Seamless loop, steady to the last bar, no fade-out. No vocals with words, no lyrics.
